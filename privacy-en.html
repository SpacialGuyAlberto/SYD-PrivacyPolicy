Origin of Data and Legal Bases

We receive data from your inputs, from your use of the app, and from the device permissions you grant. In the event of a user report, we may receive information from the reporting individual or a competent authority.

Insofar as we require data to provide your account or perform a service requested by you, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR serves as the legal basis. Processing operations dependent on consent are performed pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. We fulfill statutory obligations on the basis of Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

To protect against abuse, handle general inquiries, and defend or establish legitimate legal claims, we process necessary data based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We explain the respective legitimate interest in connection with the specific processing activity. Insofar as special categories of data are concerned, authorization under Art. 9 GDPR is additionally required.

To document your consents and their revocation, we process account affiliation, timestamp, the relevant processing operation, and the version of the declaration displayed at that time. This serves our accountability obligations under Art. 5(2) and Art. 7(1) GDPR on the basis of Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR. This documentation does not authorize any further use of underlying profile, location, or communication data for a revoked purpose.

Profile and Sensitive Search Preferences

For your profile and encounter matching, we process the photos you upload, your displayed first name, and your age, as well as your specified gender, preferred genders, age range, and relationship goals. These preferences are matched against corresponding parameters of other active users.

From your search preferences, their correlation with your own gender, and mutual interactions, conclusions may be drawn regarding your sexual orientation. This information constitutes special category data under the protection of Art. 9 GDPR. We process this data for matching algorithms and profile display strictly on the basis of your explicit consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR.

Your consent encompasses the processing of necessary search, matching, and connection parameters, as well as making designated profile details visible to compatible users for encounter purposes. It specifically excludes any use for advertising or publication outside the application.

You may revoke your consent at any time with future effect, either via the in-app privacy settings or by contacting us directly. Without this processing, encounter matchmaking based on your preferences cannot function. Your access to account management, purchase records, and legal rights remains fully unaffected.

The processing of general profile data is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Please refrain from uploading information concerning third parties or sensitive personal details that are not required for your user profile.

Location and Background Functionality

When you enable location permissions, we process location data necessary for Single-Pass functionality. This includes your current location, the reference point of your designated active area, selected radius, recording timestamps, and configured dwell time. From this data, your presence and visibility status as well as spatial proximity for encounters are determined.

For matchmaking with other individuals, relevant location and status coordinates are processed within our secure technical systems. Processing is based upon your prior, revocable consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Insofar as location metrics are correlated with attributes revealing sexual orientation to generate encounter data, your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR additionally applies.

Foreground location access while the app is active must be distinguished from background location access. Subject to your separate, explicit permission, the automated background mode can verify whether you remain within your designated zone, exit it, or satisfy dwell times at a new destination even when the app is closed.

Becoming invisible upon leaving the designated boundary circle does not automatically imply that location checks have ceased: as long as the automatic feature remains activated and background permissions remain granted, location checks may continue to operate in anticipation of a new session. You can stop this automatic monitoring at any time by disabling the automatic switch or revoking location access in your device settings.

Other users are never shown your precise GPS coordinates or a pinpoint map marker. However, visibility within the shared discovery zone and agreeing upon a joint meeting location naturally allow inferences regarding approximate proximity. Your profile may also appear in the vicinity of your home address if you activate the feature while there.

You can revoke location permissions at any time via your device settings and turn off automatic mode inside the app. Without location permission, location-dependent features cannot be accessed. Without background permission, automatic presence detection outside active app usage is restricted. Revocations apply with future effect; records of concluded actions are handled pursuant to the retention rules detailed below.

Visibility and Encounters

Compatible and authorized users are presented with the profile details designated for encounter discovery, specifically your photos, first name, and age. Your email address and complete date of birth are strictly private and never publicly visible.

To orchestrate encounter workflows, we process activation states, private favoriting actions, temporal stamps, mutual matches, proposed meetup locations, approvals, and terminations. Unilateral favoriting remains entirely secret from the other person unless a mutual match is achieved. A chosen meeting point and mutual confirmations are displayed to both participating users.

Blocklist data is utilized to prevent reciprocal profile visibility and communication within the app. Applying a user block does not, in and of itself, generate an automatic violation report to our safety and moderation team.

The legal basis for processing user-initiated app interactions is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Insofar as interactions allow inferences regarding sexual orientation, explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR is additionally required. Location-based processing complies with the dedicated terms in Section 5.

Retention Period and Account Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary to accomplish the respective operational purpose or comply with statutory retention terms. The following specific criteria apply:

  • Account and Profile Data: Maintained for the duration of the contractual agreement. Following account deletion, profile presentation and match generation cease immediately; unnecessary records are permanently erased, subject to statutory preservation duties or unresolved dispute claims.
  • Authentication Codes: Expire immediately upon redemption or preset timeout. Active session data is preserved strictly during session validity or ongoing safety investigations.
  • Location, Proximity, and Dwell Data: Utilized strictly for the real-time presence and encounter process and essential connectivity. Exact geolocation coordinates never form part of a permanent profile record and are promptly overwritten or deleted once no longer needed.
  • Favorites, Matches, and Meeting Points: Kept for orchestrating the active encounter workflow. Ongoing retention occurs strictly if an active support inquiry, moderation incident, or legal defense necessitates evidence preservation.
  • Blocklist Entries: Maintained as long as the mutual block remains active. Once deactivated, records are deleted unless required for lingering safety and abuse prevention.
  • Technical Security Logs: Preserved only as long as necessary to identify, trace, and remedy system defects or cyber attacks. In the absence of an incident, log entries are not aggregated into chronic user profiles.
  • Customer Support and Incident Tickets: Deleted upon incident resolution once no longer needed for dispute resolution or asserting legal rights. Continued storage is restricted to strictly necessary audit facts.
  • Consent and Revocation Documentation: Preserved solely to satisfy statutory accountability rules or defend against legal claims; raw chats, precise locations, or full profile data are excluded from this record.
  • Purchases and Subscription Records: Managed to enforce subscription entitlements, support purchase restoration, and handle refunds. Tax-relevant and commercial accounting receipts are governed by statutory retention periods defined by law.

Where records are preserved solely to comply with statutory duties or ongoing litigation, they are segregated and restricted from everyday processing. Backup archives may retain copies until regular scheduled lifecycle rotation; backup data is never restored into active production environments without re-applying deletion notices.

You can execute account deletion directly within the mobile application settings or via written email request. To prevent fraudulent deletions, we may require reasonable confirmation of identity. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel recurring in-app subscriptions managed by Apple or Google; details are outlined in our Terms of Service.

Your Rights

Under statutory legal requirements, you hold the right to obtain access to your personal data, rectification of inaccurate entries, erasure ('right to be forgotten'), restriction of processing, and data portability. Insofar as any processing is conditioned on your consent, you are entitled to withdraw that consent at any time with future effect. The lawfulness of prior processing remains unaffected.

Right to Object: Where data processing is founded upon Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests), you have the right to object on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will terminate processing of the affected data unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for processing that override your interests, fundamental rights, and freedoms, or where the processing serves the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims. You may object to data processing for direct marketing purposes at any time without stating reasons.

Please address your request to info@monalisaentertainment.com or via our postal address. We will respond within one month as standard. If statutory criteria warrant a deadline extension, we will notify you within that initial month regarding the extension and underlying reasons. In cases of reasonable doubt regarding identity, we may request supplementary information necessary for authentication.

Necessity and Automated Matching

You are under no legal obligation to register an account. However, without providing the mandatory information for onboarding and authentication, we cannot establish an operational account. If data or device permissions required for specific features are withheld, those features will be disabled or impaired. You may decline optional features at your discretion.

The algorithmic matching of age ranges, search preferences, spatial proximity, and activation status runs autonomously and may constitute profiling within the definition of the GDPR. Its function is to calculate which user profiles are candidates for spontaneous encounters. The sovereign choice to favorite a user and consent to an in-person meeting remains exclusively with the human users involved. This automated matching does not constitute an automated decision producing legal effects or similarly significant ramifications under Art. 22(1) GDPR.
